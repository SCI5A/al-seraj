# PRIVACY-AUDIT

## Current technical behavior

The Arabic and English contact pages validate required fields in the browser only. The homepage form follows the same local-only behavior. No CRM, SMTP, WhatsApp, external lead provider, analytics, advertising tag, cookie-consent service, database, or local persistence is active in the candidate artifact.

## Accuracy

The Arabic and English privacy pages describe the no-send/no-store behavior and explicitly mark legal review as required. They do not claim processors, analytics, cookies, CRM, or email processing that are not technically active.

## Remaining review

A human legal/content owner must review the wording before production approval. Any future provider, analytics, cookies, or data retention requires a new technical and privacy update before activation.
